Site Day

Tape first, then Magicplan, then damage photos. Print and carry this. Priorities are already decided — do not improvise the order.

What today is actually for

This phone has no LiDAR, so it cannot produce the depth archives the pipeline consumes. Photos taken today will not feed the reconstruction. What today produces is the Magicplan head‑to‑head comparison, made defensible by tape-measuring the same rooms. That is the deliverable — do not spend the day on the 8‑pass photo protocol instead.

Do this in the first 10 minutes, indoors

  • Install Magicplan and confirm the free tier can actually export a plan (PDF, PNG or DXF)
  • If it cannot, screenshot the limit message — that is itself the honest Part 3 finding
  • Note the app version
  • Clear phone storage, battery 100%, airplane mode on

The three priorities

1 — TAPE 2 ROOMS, THEN MAGICPLAN THEMthe deliverable · ~70 min

Pick living room and master bedroom. If either has no POP / false ceiling, prefer it — otherwise ceiling height is ambiguous by 10–20 cm and the comparison is worthless. Tape each room before scanning it, then scan it. Full list on page 2.

2 — DAMAGE PHOTOSonly if damage exists · 15 min

Per item: 5 overlapping close shots at 0.5–0.8 m in focus filling the frame, 1 wide shot for context, and a tape in frame for scale. That last one is what makes it evidence rather than a snapshot.

3 — 8-PASS PHOTO SURVEYlow value today · optional

Only if you are well ahead. Nothing shipped consumes phone photos. If you do it, follow the printed capture protocol exactly — and remember 1.00 m and 1.80 m are distances from the wall, the phone stays at 1.40 m.

Skip entirely

Bathrooms, balcony, utility — mirror‑heavy and lowest value per minute. Video (nothing consumes it yet). Chasing texture on blank painted walls.

Tape first

These numbers are what Magicplan will be judged against. If they are wrong, the comparison is worthless — so precision beats speed.

Technique — non-negotiable

  • Write the room name on the card before you measure. Never measure an unnamed room.
  • Record millimetres, not centimetres, with leading zeros: 1245, not 1 245.
  • Pull the tape taut and straight. A sagging tape reads short, a twisted one reads long. Sight along it.
  • Read at eye level, directly above the tape, never at an angle.
  • Measure every wall length twice, independently, and write both numbers. If they differ by more than 5 mm, measure a third time and keep all three.
  • Photograph the card after every five readings, filling the frame, so each number stays permanently linked to its room.

Then Magicplan, then evidence

Scan the rooms you taped, and nothing else. A scan of an untaped room adds nothing, because there is nothing to check it against.

Magicplan, per room

  • Stand near the doorway first and take one wide shot for context
  • Walk the perimeter slowly, one continuous sweep — do not stop and restart
  • Sweep the floor and the ceiling separately, not only the walls
  • Walk through every doorway into the next space; the opening width is the tightest measurement in the whole assessment
  • Keep the phone upright, screen facing you, move your feet — do not wave it around
  • Do one room to completion before starting the next

Export and evidence — do not skip

  • Export the plan (PDF, PNG or DXF — whatever the tier allows) and save it off the phone. Cloud-only is a real risk of losing the day’s work.
  • Screenshot every export-limit message. “Free tier cannot export” is a legitimate, reportable Part 3 result and needs evidence.
  • Note the handicap plainly: this phone has no LiDAR, so Magicplan ran its camera/SLAM path. State it wherever the numbers appear.
  • Magicplan is another estimate, never ground truth. The tape is the reference.

Before you leave the flat — final 10 minutes

  • Both tape cards photographed, room name legible
  • Every tape number written in millimetres, wall lengths measured twice
  • Magicplan exports for both rooms saved off the phone
  • Export-limit screenshots saved
  • Damage shots taken with a tape in frame, if any damage exists
  • Nothing sent through WhatsApp, Instagram or any compressor
  • Everything backed up to a second location

Cutoffs

Tape both rooms before scanning either. If you scan first and run out of time, the measurements never exist and the whole session is wasted.

Absolute stop when the site closes. A room measured but not scanned still yields a usable number; a room scanned but not measured yields nothing checkable. A declared gap costs far less than a rushed, broken capture.
